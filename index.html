Application identity

One recognizable icon across supported surfaces.

The Suite website uses the reviewed canonical application identity for each product. Web/PWA, Android, desktop/Linux, future iOS, launchers, installers, updates, recovery tools, documentation, and repository surfaces may use platform-specific derivatives, but those derivatives must preserve the same underlying product identity.

Third-party infrastructure marks are separate. They identify the external technology being referenced and are not GoreeCloud application icons.
